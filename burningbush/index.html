Turn numbers into pictures

Each digit maps to a consonant sound. Vowels are free glue. String the sounds into a word, and the word becomes a picture.

0s, z  "z" starts Zero
1t, d  one downstroke
2n  two downstrokes
3m  three downstrokes
4r  "fou-R"
5L  Roman L = 50
6j, sh, ch, soft g
7k, hard c/g  two 7s = K
8f, v  cursive f ≈ 8
9p, b  mirror 9/P
The one rule: a peg word may contain only the sounds it stands for. dish = d·sh = 16 ✓, but shark = sh·r·k = 647 ✗. Vowels and w, h, y are silent glue.
