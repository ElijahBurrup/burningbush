Three pictures, one scene

The whole method, and it is older than you think — the Major System has been used to memorise numbers since the seventeenth century.

ONE

Numbers become sounds

Every digit gets a consonant. Nothing to memorise beyond ten of them, and they never change.

0 is s, 1 is t or d, 2 is n.

TWO

Sounds become pictures

Those consonants make a word, and the word is a thing you can see. A number you could not hold becomes an object you can.

01 is Seed. 02 is Sun. 40 is a Rose.

THREE

Pictures become a scene

Put the book, the chapter and the verse together into one strange moment, and leave it in a room you already walk through.

John 3:16 lives somewhere in your own kitchen.
