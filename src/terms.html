Refunds

If Burning Bush is not what you hoped, write to us within 30 days of paying and we will refund you. You do not have to explain yourself. After 30 days we will still listen, and will usually say yes if something has gone wrong at our end.

If you bought through the App Store or Google Play, their refund process applies instead of ours, because they took the payment rather than us.

Group licences

  • One licence is five seats for a year. You get a code, and you choose what it says.
  • A seat is taken by the first five accounts to enter the code, and each keeps Pro until the licence term ends.
  • You can take a seat back at any time from your owner dashboard — if somebody stops using it, that seat can go to somebody who will.
  • A seat that has been taken back cannot be reassigned instantly, and there is a limit on how many times seats can be moved within one term. Both exist so a five-seat licence stays a five-seat licence rather than becoming an unlimited one passed around a congregation.

Giving

You can pay for other people to use Burning Bush. That money goes into a fund and is given out to people who ask for help with the cost. Two honest things about it:

  • It is not tax-deductible. Kingdom Builders AI is not a charity, and we will not pretend it is one.
  • Gifts are not refundable, because the money is given away to somebody else.

Comp codes

A code we give you unlocks Pro for a limited period and then stops. It is a loan rather than a gift, and we say so at the point you enter it.
